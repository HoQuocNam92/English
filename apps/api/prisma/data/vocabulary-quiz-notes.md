# Vocabulary quiz meanings

The quiz dataset is separate from full dictionary definitions and learner progress. The current catalog contains 2,589 entries: 2,310 have a short meaning suitable for choices. 38 entries have edited short meanings (including term case variants). Other meanings use an existing complete first sentence of at most 180 characters; they have not all been manually rewritten. Cross references are resolved where their target has a usable short meaning. No sentence is cut mid-word or at an arbitrary character limit.

279 entries still need editorial review and are listed in vocabulary-quiz-review.json. They remain available for study but are omitted from multiple-choice generation. New entries can use short Vietnamese definitions; unresolved references and oversized definitions are excluded.

Feedback keeps all choices visible: the correct choice is green and the selected wrong choice is red. A short Vietnamese explanation is hidden until the learner opens “Xem chi tiết”. Full English glossary text and explanations of unselected choices are not displayed.
